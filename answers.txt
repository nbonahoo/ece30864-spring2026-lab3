1. Pros of agile development: Timely delivery and adaptability.
   Cons of agile development: variable goals and lack of documentation.

2. Scrum Master: facilitating key scrum events and fostering a high 
   performing, self managing team environment.
   Product Owner: Setting goals, prioritizing features for development, 
   and communicating with stakeholders.

3. The three types of meetings are Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, and Sprint Review.
   Sprint Planning: Determine sprint goals and select tasks from the product backlog.
   Daily Scrum: Track progress and identify blockers.
   Sprint review: gather feedback and adjust backlog.
